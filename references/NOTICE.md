# Attribution & license

This skill is a thin local wrapper around the **HyperFrames** `frame.md` design
system, created by the **HyperFrames / HeyGen** team. All of the underlying
method and material is theirs:

- the `frame.md` format and the `frame.md → design.md → DESIGN.md` resolution
  contract,
- the `frame-presets` template library,
- the composition grammar (the "Frame Craft Bar" gates, the cqw container law,
  fit-to-measure typography, the six-treatment recipe, the pre-render
  self-audit),
- the `frame-showcase.html` contact-sheet idea.

**Source:** https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes (`skills/hyperframes-creative/`).

material locally.

## The full library
HyperFrames ships **13** presets (biennale-yellow, blockframe, blue-professional,
bold-poster, broadside, capsule, cartesian, claude, cobalt-grid, coral,
creative-mode, daisy-days, editorial-forest) plus an interactive design-picker.
Only four are vendored here; pull any other preset, or the latest versions,
straight from the upstream repo or the `hyperframes-creative` skill.
